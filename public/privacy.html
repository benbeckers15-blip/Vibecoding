SipLocal

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 June 2026

SipLocal is a wine-tourism guide for Tasmania, published as an iOS app by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information the app collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it. We try to keep the wording plain — if anything is unclear, email benjamin@siplocalguide.com and we'll explain.

Who we are

SipLocal is operated by Benjamin Beckers, based in Australia. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at benjamin@siplocalguide.com.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to give you an account and a useful guide to Tasmanian wineries, plus usage analytics that tell us which parts of the app people actually use.
  • We never sell or rent your personal information, and we don't run third-party advertising in the app.
  • We do share aggregated, de-identified insights — for example, "interest in sparkling wine rose this month" — with the wineries and regional partners we work with. These insights are statistics about groups of users; they never identify you, and we never share your raw messages, your identity, or your contact details. Details below.
  • Our concierge chat sends your typed questions to AI vendors (OpenAI, Google, Voyage AI, Upstash) for processing — never your account email or identity.
  • You can delete your account, and everything stored about you, from inside the app at any time.

What we collect and why

Account information

When you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide. Email is used to sign you in and to send service-related messages (e.g. password reset). We do not send marketing email. Password handling is delegated to Firebase Authentication; we never see the plaintext password.

Profile information

The profile screen lets you optionally set a display name and a home city. These are stored against your account and shown only to you.

Your activity in the app

We store the actions you take that are meant to persist across sessions: wineries and articles you save, custom lists you create, tasting notes you record, trips you plan, and your concierge chat history while you are logged in. This data is yours; you can view, edit, and delete it from inside the app.

Usage analytics

We record how the app is used: which screens you visit, which buttons you tap, which wineries, articles, and trips you open, and which search filters and suggested prompts you use. Each event carries a timestamp and an identifier (your account ID when you're signed in, or an anonymous installation ID otherwise). We use this data to understand which features are working, fix what isn't, and produce the aggregated insights described below. We do not use it for third-party advertising, and we do not share event-level analytics data with anyone.

Precise location

If you grant location permission, we use your current GPS coordinates to (a) sort wineries by drive time when you ask for a trip plan, and (b) anchor "near me" answers in the concierge. We do not store your location after the request that needed it has completed. You can revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings; the app continues to work without it (we just can't offer "near me" ordering).

Concierge chat content

Messages you type into the in-app concierge are sent to our server, where they are processed by the AI vendors listed below to produce a reply. We retain chat transcripts on our server — associated with a session identifier, and with your account ID when you are signed in — for the period described under Retention. We use them to operate and improve the concierge (debugging wrong answers, measuring quality and cost) and, after removing identifying details, to build the aggregated insights described in the next section. Please don't include sensitive personal information in concierge messages — they are wine-tourism questions, and we treat them that way.

Technical and diagnostic data

Our server logs requests it receives — IP address, timestamp, HTTP status — for security and abuse prevention. The Firebase SDK generates an anonymous installation ID and an authentication token while you are signed in. We do not run third-party advertising SDKs, and analytics data stays within the services listed in this policy.

Aggregated insights we share with winery partners

SipLocal is funded in part by the wineries and regional partners we work with. We provide them with aggregated, de-identified insights about how visitors engage with the region and with their own listing — for example: the share of concierge questions that mention sparkling wine, the most-requested visit days, how often a winery's profile was viewed this month, or common themes in what travellers ask about ("looking for child-friendly cellar doors near Hobart").

Before anything reaches a partner, we apply all of the following:

  • De-identification. Names, email addresses, contact details, and any other identifying information are removed. Insights are derived from chat content and usage events only after this step.
  • Aggregation. Partners receive statistics, trends, and summarised themes about groups of users — never an individual user's profile, chat transcript, location history, or browsing trail.
  • Minimum group sizes. We do not report on segments so small that an individual could reasonably be picked out of them.
  • No re-identification. Our agreements prohibit partners from attempting to re-identify anyone from the insights we provide.

To be explicit about what this is not: we never sell or share your personal information — your identity, your contact details, your individual messages, or your individual activity — with wineries or anyone else. If you'd prefer your data to be excluded even from anonymous aggregation, email us (see Your rights) and we will exclude your account.

Third parties that process your data

To run SipLocal we rely on a small number of third-party services. Each of them acts as a processor for us under their own privacy policies and security commitments:

Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Hosting)

Stores your account credentials, profile, and activity. Hosts the server function that runs the concierge. Operated by Google LLC. See the Firebase privacy policy.

OpenAI

Processes the text of your concierge messages for question routing and interpretation. We send only your typed question and the relevant winery context — not your email, name, or any identifier that links the message back to you in OpenAI's logs. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

Google Gemini

Generates the concierge's replies from your question and the retrieved winery context. Receives the conversation text but no account information. Operated by Google LLC. See Google's privacy policy.

Langfuse

Our observability service for the concierge: it stores traces of concierge conversations (messages, replies, timing, and cost data, grouped by session and — when signed in — account ID) so we can debug wrong answers and monitor quality. See Langfuse's privacy policy.

Voyage AI

Generates the vector embeddings our concierge uses for semantic retrieval. Receives your concierge question as input; receives no account information. See Voyage AI's privacy policy.

Upstash Vector

Hosts the vector database the concierge queries. Receives the embedding vector and a metadata filter; never receives raw chat text or account information. See Upstash's privacy policy.

Google Maps Platform

Renders map tiles on the device and answers driving-time queries from our server. Receives the coordinates being mapped or routed. See Google's privacy policy.

Beyond these processors and the aggregated partner insights described above, we do not share your personal information with any other third parties, and we do not run advertising networks in the app.

Where your data is stored

Our Firebase project and Cloud Functions run in Google's us-central1 region. Vector data lives in Upstash's cloud region of your data centre. Map tile requests are routed to Google's nearest edge. If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or California, the international transfer of your data to these services is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses and the vendors' own transfer mechanisms.

How long we keep your data

  • Account, profile, saved items, lists, tastings, trips: kept until you delete them or close your account.
  • Concierge chat history: kept in your device's memory during the session. Server-side transcripts (in our observability tooling) are retained for up to 12 months in identifiable form for quality and debugging purposes, after which they are deleted or fully de-identified. Aggregated, de-identified statistics derived from them may be kept indefinitely — they no longer relate to you.
  • Usage analytics events: retained for up to 24 months, then deleted or de-identified.
  • Server logs: Cloud Functions execution logs are retained for up to 30 days for debugging and abuse-prevention purposes.
  • OpenAI / Voyage / Upstash processing logs: retained by those vendors according to their own policies (typically 30 days for OpenAI's abuse-monitoring window, opted out of training).

Your rights

You can exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access: view all your saved data in the app's Profile and Trips screens.
  • Correction: edit your name, home city, and any of your saved content directly in the app.
  • Deletion: delete individual items from inside the app, or delete your entire account from the Profile screen — this removes your authentication record, profile, and all associated content. Aggregated statistics computed before deletion can't be un-aggregated, but they contain nothing that identifies you.
  • Portability: request a machine-readable export of your data by emailing benjamin@siplocalguide.com; we'll respond within 30 days.
  • Objection / opt-out: email us to object to a particular use of your data — including having your data excluded from the aggregated partner insights described above.
  • Complaints: if you are in the EU/UK, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in Australia, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Children

SipLocal is intended for users aged 17 or older, in line with our App Store age rating for alcohol-related content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections between the app and our server are encrypted using HTTPS. Firestore Security Rules are configured so that only your account can read or modify your own user document and its subcollections. API keys for our third-party services live in server-side secret storage, not on your device. No security model is perfect; please tell us promptly if you suspect a vulnerability.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when we make material changes to how the app handles your data, and we'll update the effective date at the top. If the change is significant — for example, a new third-party processor or a new category of data — we'll also notify you inside the app the next time you open it.
