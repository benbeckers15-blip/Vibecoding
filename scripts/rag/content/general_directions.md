Tasmania is served by flights into Hobart and Launceston from Melbourne and Sydney, with Hobart the more common entry point for visitors focused on the south and East Coast, Launceston the natural base for the Tamar Valley and Pipers River. A hire car is effectively mandatory for wine tourism — public transport between cellar doors is nonexistent, and the distances between regions make rideshare impractical for a full day out. Book the car before you book anything else, particularly in summer when availability tightens quickly. An international driving licence is accepted. Roads are generally good, but the midlands highway is deceptively fast and rural roads to smaller cellar doors can be narrow and unsealed. GPS is reliable in most areas, though mobile coverage drops in parts of the Huon Valley and far East Coast. Ferry from Melbourne via Spirit of Tasmania docks in Devonport on the north coast — a scenic option that allows you to bring your own vehicle, though the crossing takes nine to eleven hours overnight.
