## Founding & History
Apogee Tasmania was established in 2007 by Dr. Andrew Pirie. The Apogee wine brand itself was launched in 2010. The name "Apogee" signifies "the highest point". Dr. Pirie's extensive university climate research was instrumental in the establishment of the initial vineyards in the Piper Brook/Pipers River area, including the Pipers Brook Vineyard, which he co-founded with his brother David in 1974. After managing Pipers Brook for 30 years, he departed in 2003. A significant milestone for Apogee was being awarded Tasmanian Vineyard of the Year in 2013.

## People
The current owner, founder, and principal winemaker of Apogee Tasmania is Dr. Andrew Pirie. Dr. Pirie is recognized as the recipient of Australia's first PhD in viticulture. His career in the Australian wine industry spans five decades. In recognition of his contributions to the Tasmanian wine and tourism industries, he was awarded a Member of the Order of Australia (AM) in 2001. Beyond his hands-on role at Apogee, Dr. Pirie consults on industry development, conducts climate research as an Honorary Researcher at the Tasmanian Institute of Agriculture, and is currently writing a book on wine terroir.

## Vineyard & Site
Apogee Tasmania is situated near Lebrina in the Pipers River district of northern Tasmania. The vineyard encompasses 2 hectares (2ha), a size comparable to the average vineyard in France's Champagne region. The site is characterized by deep ferrosols, which effectively supply moisture to the vines throughout the growing season. The average growing season temperature at the vineyard is 14.5 degrees Celsius. The majority of the vineyard is planted with Pinot Noir (62%), Chardonnay (16%), Pinot Gris (20%), and a small amount of Pinot Meunier (2%). Apogee employs a unique unilateral SH trellis system, which is designed to increase leaf area for optimal sunshine capture. This system utilizes a 2.2 x 1.3 meter spacing, resulting in a vine density of approximately 3,600 vines per hectare. The farming approach at Apogee focuses on producing healthy fruit with minimal intervention, guided by a scientific understanding of the vineyard's ecology.

## Wines
Apogee Tasmania produces both traditional method sparkling wines and still wines, all of which are single vineyard expressions from the estate. The varietals cultivated and produced include Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Pinot Meunier, and Pinot Gris. Notable wines from the property include APOGEE Deluxe Vintage Brut, APOGEE Deluxe Vintage Rose, Alto Pinot Noir, and Pinot Gris, with the latter two released under the Alto label. The winemaking philosophy prioritizes intensity and hands-on quality over scale, aiming to produce high-quality wines from a small, meticulously tended area. 
## Cellar Door Experience
Apogee Tasmania's cellar door operates strictly by appointment. Visitors can experience a tutored tasting that features four single vineyard wines from the property. The cellar door is located within an historic farm cottage on the estate. A distinctive aspect of the visit is the opportunity to observe a hand disgorging demonstration. Booking in advance is recommended to ensure a personalized experience.

## Awards & Recognition
Apogee Tasmania was named Tasmanian Vineyard of the Year in 2013. Specific wine recognitions include the Apogee Alto Pinot Gris 2021, which received 95 points from Huon Hooke of The Real Review. The Apogee Single Vineyard Deluxe Vintage Brut 2017 and the Apogee Single Vineyard Alto Pinot Noir 2020 also both received 95 points from Huon Hooke, The Real Review, in February 2022. Dr. Andrew Pirie's earlier work also garnered significant awards; his 1994 Ninth Island Chardonnay won the white wine trophy at the International Wine Challenge in London, and his Pirie Estate Riesling 2007 was awarded Best White Wine in Sweden by System Bolaget in 2011. He was also a finalist for the QANTAS/Australian Gourmet Traveller Winemaker of the Year Award in both 2002 and 2003.

## Notable / Distinctive
Apogee Tasmania is distinctive due to its founder, Dr. Andrew Pirie, who holds Australia's first PhD in viticulture and has been a highly influential figure in the Tasmanian wine industry for decades. Dr. Pirie was a pioneer in the Australian wine industry, establishing the first close-planted vineyards in 1974. The vineyard's small scale of 2 hectares is notable, being equivalent to the average vineyard size in Champagne, which underscores a philosophy focused on intensity and quality rather than volume. The winery utilizes a unique APOGEE unilateral SH trellis system. All sparkling wines are 100% grown and disgorged on the property. A core objective of Apogee is to produce traditional method sparkling wines that can rival those from Champagne. Furthermore, Dr. Pirie's Pirie sparkling wine has been served to Queen Elizabeth II on at least two occasions.
