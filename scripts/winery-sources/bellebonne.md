## Founding & History
Bellebonne was established in 2014. Some sources indicate 2015 as the founding year. The winery was founded by Natalie Fryar with the specific aim of creating Australia's finest sparkling wine. Prior to establishing Bellebonne, Natalie Fryar began her career in sparkling wine at Seppelts Great Western winery in 1996 and subsequently spent 14 years as a senior sparkling winemaker and brand ambassador at Jansz. The name "Bellebonne" translates to "beautiful and good," reflecting a philosophy that good things should embody beauty and purpose.

## People
The founder and principal winemaker of Bellebonne is Natalie Fryar. She is widely recognized as "Australia's Sparkling Queen". Fryar's journey in the wine industry commenced at the age of 14 with work experience at Hardys Reynella winery, followed by her first vintage two years later. She is married to Hugh McCullough, who is the winemaker for Wellington & Wolfe. Beyond Bellebonne, Natalie Fryar is also a co-founder of Abel Gin Company and provides consultancy services to other wine labels, including Pipers Brook Vineyard and Ninth Island. She has also played a significant role as a consultant for the Australian Cuvee Centre.

## Vineyard & Site
Bellebonne sources its fruit from carefully selected high-altitude vineyards across Tasmania, primarily within the Tamar Valley, Coal River Valley, and Pipers River regions. The Pipers River area is considered optimal for sparkling wine production due to its specific latitude, maritime climate, frost-free conditions, extended cool and even growing seasons, cold nights, and high cloud cover, which results in low UV radiation. These environmental factors contribute to the development of thin grape skins and a high acid ratio, both essential for producing high-quality sparkling base wines. The Tamar Valley, Tasmania's oldest wine region, is characterized by its cool maritime climate and iron-rich soils. The primary varietals utilized for Bellebonne's sparkling wines, grown in these regions, include Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, and Pinot Meunier. Natalie Fryar's winemaking philosophy emphasizes light pressing to extract only free-run juice for the base wines, avoiding oxidative handling or excessive oak influence to preserve the fruit's inherent purity, elegance, and complexity. The winery crafts its cuvées in small quantities, utilizing grapes from specific grower vines in Pipers River. Bellebonne adheres to traditional méthode champenoise practices, which involve hand-picking, whole-bunch pressing, extended lees aging, and minimal dosage. Since January 2023, Bellebonne has established a permanent home at Evenfall Wines in Legana, located near Launceston. This 7.2-hectare site was originally planted as Elmslie Winery in the mid-1970s. The vineyard at the Evenfall site is managed by Matthias Utzinger.

## Cellar Door Experience
Bellebonne operates a cellar door at Evenfall Wines, situated at 2 Upper McEwans Road, Legana. The location is approximately 15 minutes from Launceston. The cellar door is open from Wednesday to Sunday, between 10:30 AM and 4:00 PM. Another source states 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and another 10:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Visitors have the opportunity to taste Bellebonne's cuvées, as well as wines from Evenfall and Natalie Fryar's husband's label, Wellington & Wolfe. A "Bellebonne Brunch" experience is available, which includes a tasting of Bis Blanc and Bis Rosé, a complimentary tasting of an exclusive Vintage wine paired with blinis, and a small brunch menu featuring local delicacies. This experience also offers a $15 credit towards a two-bottle purchase as part of an "Off Season Offer". The cellar door is described as a cottage with a verandah that provides views over the vineyards. It is noted as being family-friendly and pet-friendly, with inquiries recommended for the latter.

## Awards & Recognition
Bellebonne has garnered significant acclaim. In 2022, the winery received the award for Best Sparkling Rosé in Australia at the Halliday Wine Awards. Renowned wine writer Tyson Stelzer stated in 2021 that no sparkling wine brand introduced in Australia in recent years has been as exceptional as Bellebonne. Bellebonne is one of only two wineries in Australia to have been awarded 7 stars by Tyson Stelzer. Stelzer also recognized Bellebonne's 2017 'blanc de blancs' as Sparkling White of the Year (2024), assigning it a score of 96 points out of 100. The 2019 Bellebonne Vintage Rosé was named Best Sparkling Rosé at the 2022 Halliday Awards. The Real Review indicates Bellebonne has received 7 Gold Ribbons and 4 Silver Ribbons. Specific wines have achieved high ratings, such as the Bellebonne Blanc de Blancs Chardonnay Vintage 2017 and 2015, both ranked #1 in their respective categories for Australian Sparkling blanc de blancs. The Bellebonne Vintage Cuvée 2021 was awarded 95 gold, and the Bellebonne Blanc de Blancs 2019 received 96+ gold from the Halliday Wine Companion. The Bellebonne Vintage Rosé Pinot Noir 2021 was ranked #2 among 25 Australian Sparkling rosés from 2021. The Bellebonne Bis NV Rosé received 92 points from Huon Hooke and 93 points from Tyson Stelzer.

## Notable / Distinctive
Bellebonne is Natalie Fryar's personal project, established with the singular ambition of producing Australia's finest sparkling wine. Natalie Fryar is widely acclaimed as "Australia's Sparkling Queen". Her winemaking philosophy centers on capturing the pristine fruit power and delicate elegance inherent in Tasmanian produce, crafting wines that highlight the fruit's natural beauty, fostering longevity and complexity. Fryar holds a strong belief that Tasmania possesses a sparkling wine pedigree comparable to Champagne. She advocates for collegiality within the sparkling wine industry, collaborating with notable winemakers such as Andrew Pirie and Ed Carr. The winery's tagline, "made by Tasmania," underscores the belief that the island's unique character profoundly influences both the terroir and the resulting wine. Bellebonne's wines are handcrafted in small batches, employing traditional methods and minimal intervention. The winery's permanent location at Evenfall Wines, a site with historical plantings dating back to the mid-1970s, facilitates a distinctive collaboration with Evenfall and Wellington & Wolfe wines, offering a restaurant that features local produce and panoramic views of the Tamar River.
