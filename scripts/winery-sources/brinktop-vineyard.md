## Founding & History
Brinktop Vineyard was established in 2017 by Todd Goebel and Gill Christian. Prior to founding Brinktop, Goebel and Christian had been cultivating grapes and producing wine in the Coal River Valley since 1999. In 2017, they relocated across the valley to establish their new vineyard on the rocky slopes of the Killara farm. The Killara property had been farmed for decades by the Iles family before their arrival. Before establishing Brinktop, the couple owned and operated Coal Valley Vineyard, which was originally the Johnstone family's 1-hectare Treehouse Vineyard outside Cambridge, later expanded to 4.5 hectares, and sold in 2017.

## People
Brinktop Vineyard is owned and operated by Todd Goebel and Gill Christian. Todd Goebel is involved in winemaking, and both he and Gill Christian welcome visitors at the cellar door.

## Vineyard & Site
The vineyard was established in 2017 on the Killara Farm at Penna. Initially, the purchase included a house with a 1-hectare vineyard. The current Brinktop Vineyard spans 8 hectares. The site features rocky slopes with an east-north-east aspect, which helps protect grapes from harsh afternoon sun. The soils are described as mineral-rich dolerite. Summer afternoon sea breezes from nearby Frederick Henry Bay contribute to cooling the site, aiding in the retention of perfume and freshness in the wines. Varietals planted include Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Tempranillo, and Syrah. The farming approach emphasizes sustainability, focusing on building a relationship with the vineyard through the seasons. Wines are made from hand-picked fruit and carefully selected clones.

## Cellar Door Experience
The cellar door is open on Friday and Saturday afternoons from 12 noon to 5 pm. It operates seasonally, typically open from September to May and closed for a winter break from June to August. Visits can also be arranged by appointment. Wine tastings are described as relaxed and informative, with the possibility of including hands-on winemaking experiences for interested visitors. Visitors are welcomed by winemaker Todd Goebel and co-owner Gill Christian. The tasting experience takes place in a rustic setting with views over the vines and the Coal Valley, and depending on the season, winemaking activities might be visible. Visitors have the opportunity to "meet the maker".
