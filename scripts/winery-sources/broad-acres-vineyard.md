## Founding & History
Broad Acres Vineyard was established in 2016 in the North West of Tasmania, Australia. The vineyard continues traditions of quality and a hands-on approach that were initially set by Mike and Gaynor Broadby in 1983.

## Vineyard & Site
Broad Acres Vineyard is a coastal vineyard situated in a cool maritime climate in the North West of Tasmania, ideally located between Ulverstone and Devonport on the Cradle Coast. It benefits from its proximity to Bass Strait to the North and Tasmania's highland mountains to the South. The vineyard cultivates five grape varieties: Pinot Noir, Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Pinot Gris, and Riesling. ## Cellar Door Experience
Broad Acres Vineyard features an onsite cafe and cellar door named "Windows on Westella". It is open seven days a week for tastings and cellar door sales, operating from 8:30 am to 3:30 pm daily, but closed on public holidays. The vineyard also participates in the Tasmanian Mixed Dozen freight scheme, allowing visitors to ship purchased wines to their home address.

## Food & Hospitality
The "Windows on Westella" cafe is a modern, fully licensed establishment offering full table service and an extensive seasonal all-day menu.
