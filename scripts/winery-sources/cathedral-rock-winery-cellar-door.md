## Founding & History
Cathedral Rock Vineyard was established in 2004 with an initial planting of Pinot Noir vines. The vineyard expanded in 2014 with the planting of Pinot Gris, using their own cuttings. A further block of Pinot Noir, featuring three selected clones suited to the site, was planted in 2016. In 2019, a small area of Riesling was added to the vineyard.

## Vineyard & Site
Cathedral Rock Vineyard is situated beneath the majestic Cathedral Rock, nestled between Tasmania's Huon Valley and D'Entrecasteaux Channel. The vineyard spans 1.2 hectares and is considered ideal for growing cool-climate wines. The site's terroir is believed to be influenced by ancient Permian era marine fossils found within the rocks scattered throughout the vineyard. Varietals planted include Pinot Noir, Pinot Gris, Riesling, Rosé, and Syrah. The vineyard is dedicated to growing grapes that express its unique terroir.
