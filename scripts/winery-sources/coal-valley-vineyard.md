## Founding & History
The first "Old Block" of vines at Coal Valley Vineyard was planted in 1991 by Drs. Margaret and Gradon Johnston. Gillian Christian and Todd Goebel acquired the vineyard, then known as Treehouse Vineyard, in 1999, renamed it Coal Valley Vineyard, and expanded its area. The vineyard came under new management in 2017, with the previous owners deciding on a fresh start.

## People
Drs. Margaret and Gradon Johnston were the original planters of the vineyard in 1991. Gillian Christian and Todd Goebel owned the vineyard from 1999. Since 2017, the vineyard has been under new management. As of April 2026, the estate is referred to as the Nicholson family's estate.

## Vineyard & Site
The vineyard features steep slopes that face due north. Approximately 5 hectares (5 ha) are planted with vines. Other sources indicate "twelve acres" (approximately 4.85 hectares) or an expansion to 4.5 hectares. The varietals planted include Pinot Noir, Riesling, Chardonnay, Tempranillo, Cabernet Sauvignon, and Merlot. The farming approach emphasizes sustainability, with all vines being hand pruned and grapes hand-picked. The wines are made from 100% single-site estate-grown fruit. The Coal River Valley region, where the vineyard is located, is characterized by complex soil types, including Jurassic dolerite and sedimentary rock, basalt flows through alluvial sands, mudstone, and gravels, as well as heavy clay and clay-loams.
