## Founding & History
Delamere Vineyard, located in the Pipers River region of northeastern Tasmania, is one of the area's pioneering wine estates. The vineyard was first established in 1981 or 1982 by Richard and Dallas Richardson. It was among the oldest vineyards in the region at the time of its founding. The fertile soils of the property had previously been used for a fruit orchard and market garden.

Shane Holloway acquired the vineyard in 2007. His wife, Fran Austin, joined him in the Delamere Vineyard business in 2011. Together, they have expanded the plantings and developed the winery.

## People
Delamere Vineyard is owned and operated by husband-and-wife team Shane Holloway and Fran Austin. They are described as co-owner/winemakers.

Shane Holloway's formal entry into winemaking began in 2001, though his family had established a vineyard in the Adelaide Hills years prior. His background is in marine biology and aquaculture, which initially brought him to Tasmania for post-graduate research. He completed a post-graduate degree in winemaking from Adelaide University and gained experience in regions like Adelaide Hills, Yarra Valley, and Oregon (USA) before returning to Tasmania. Shane oversees vineyard management and big-picture production, including winery developments.

Fran Austin moved to Tasmania in 2002 from Western Australia to establish and manage a winery for a major Australian wine corporation. She studied winemaking at Adelaide University, applying in 1991. Her career includes vintages in Burgundy, Oregon, Yarra Valley, Adelaide Hills, and the south of France, with roles at Domaine Chandon, Petaluma, Houghton, Heemskerk, and Rochecombe. She was the head winemaker at Bay of Fires for nine years before joining Delamere in 2011. Fran was named Gourmet Traveller Young Winemaker of the Year in 2005, a Len Evans Tutorial Scholar in 2009, and a Don Martin Fellow in 2016. Fran focuses on the "big picture strategy" and planning for the business.

## Vineyard & Site
Delamere Vineyard is situated in the Pipers River region of northeastern Tasmania. The vineyard was initially planted in 1981 or 1982. It currently has 6.5 hectares of grapes established, with a total vineyard area of 11 hectares. The vineyard was doubled in size in 2013, with new plantings of four Chardonnay clones and three Pinot Noir clones.

The vineyard is planted exclusively to Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. These vines are densely arrayed at 7,500 plants per hectare. The site features rich ironstone soils, specifically deep red Ferrosol soils. These soils provide good fertility, water drainage, and moisture retention, which helps alleviate the need for irrigation. The combination of soil and site is considered well-suited for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. The vineyard uses a cane-pruned, vertical shoot position trellis system with a low fruiting wire at 60cm off the ground. The low yield per vine and reflective heat from the ground contribute to wines with superior depth of flavor and ripe tannins.

The Pipers River region benefits from a unique climatic advantage: a very low diurnal temperature range, which promotes temperature stability. This is due to the cooling effect of Bass Strait waters on hot northerly winds and the warming of cool southerlies as they cross the island. The cool temperatures and significant sunshine hours are ideal for retaining delicate aromas in the fruit. The vineyard is managed 100% by hand due to its close planting and steep, cold hills.

## Cellar Door Experience
The Delamere Vineyard cellar door is located within the winery itself. It is described as serene, relaxed, and friendly, offering a farmyard-like haven. Visitors can observe winemaking activities, such as sparkling wines being prepared for hand riddling or Pinot Noir and Chardonnay maturing in oak.

The cellar door is open Thursday to Monday from 10:30 am to 4:30 pm, and by appointment on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. It is closed on Christmas Day and Boxing Day. Bookings are preferred for tastings and are essential for groups of four or more, though walk-ins may be accommodated based on availability.

Tastings are flexible, with options to add rare block wines and museum releases to the initial lineup. Tasting flights are offered, comparing different wines in a guided format to enhance understanding. The experience aims to be informative and welcoming. The cellar door is family-friendly, with space for children to roam in sight of the tasting area. It is also dog-friendly.

## Notable / Distinctive
Delamere Vineyards is one of Tasmania's longest-running family-owned and operated wine estates. It is recognized as one of the pioneering vineyards in the Pipers Brook region, with its establishment in the early 1980s making it among the oldest in the area. The vineyard's age is considered a valuable resource in Tasmania, contributing to increased wine quality from mature vines.

The winery is a "true Tasmanian Grower Producer," meaning 100% of its wines are family-owned and run, grown, made, and bottled at their Pipers River Estate. Shane Holloway and Fran Austin are a husband-and-wife winemaking team who have significantly expanded the vineyard and production since taking over in 2007. Their cellar door is located within the working winery, allowing visitors to observe winemaking activities.
