## Founding & History
The site now known as Evenfall Winery and Restaurant was originally established in the mid-1970s by Richard Power as Powercourt Vineyard in Legana. It was later known as Elmslie Winery. Tasmanian-born berry tycoon Richard Winspear acquired the 7.2-hectare property a few years prior to late 2023. Evenfall Wines released its first vintage in 2022. The Bellebonne at Evenfall cellar door and restaurant officially opened in late 2023. Natalie Fryar and her winemaker husband, Hugh McCullough, moved into the winery full-time in January 2023.

## People
Richard Winspear is the owner of Evenfall Wines, with Ben Gourlay as a business partner. Liam McElhinney serves as the principal winemaker for Evenfall Wines and is also the head winemaker at Tasmanian Vintners. Natalie Fryar, known as 'Australia's Sparkling Queen', is a co-located collaborator and consultant for Bellebonne wines, which are also showcased at Evenfall. Her husband, Hugh McCullough, is the winemaker for Wellington & Wolfe wines, also featured at the site. Matthias Utzinger, who owns the adjoining property, is Evenfall's viticulturist. Chef Tom Kirby, formerly of Qamby Estate/Hazelbrae, leads the restaurant kitchen. The staff are described as experienced and knowledgeable, with Gabe and Leroy specifically mentioned for their service.

## Vineyard & Site
Evenfall Vineyard spans 7.2 hectares. It is situated on eastern-facing slopes overlooking the Tamar River, offering views towards the mountains. The vineyard is meticulously planted with Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and some of the oldest Cabernet vines in Tasmania. The site is recognized as one of Tasmania's founding vineyards and boasts a rich heritage. The Tamar River's moderating influence helps regulate temperature and provides humidity, shielding grapes from harsh UV rays. A small creek on the southern side of the vineyard creates a natural breeze that cools the grapes. The vineyard operates with a strong practice of holistic sustainability, aiming to minimize its environmental footprint.

## Food & Hospitality
Evenfall features a restaurant that offers modern Australian cuisine, emphasizing locally sourced Tasmanian produce with a farm-to-table ethos. The menu is seasonal and includes wood-fired cooking. Notable dishes include Pork Belly, House Gnocchi, Tea Smoked Duck, Char Grilled Porterhouse, Kingfish Ceviche, Line Caught Fish, Apple Tart Tartine, Strawberries and Cream, and Affogato. Vegan options are available and marked on the menu.

The dining experience is enhanced by a recently renovated dining room that combines intimacy with grand vineyard views. In the off-season, the restaurant features a roaring fireplace. Guests can also relax under weeping willow trees overlooking the Pinot vines or on the outdoor deck with views of the valley. Evenfall is suitable for functions and special events, offering dedicated spaces such as "The Oolong Room" for intimate gatherings and "The Willow Table" for long table lunches under the weeping willows. Both special event options feature an $89 per person seasonal share menu. A 10% surcharge applies on weekends, and a 15% surcharge on public holidays.

Evenfall is recognized as a "favourite among the region's wineries". The winery is also home to Bellebonne, whose sparkling wines are celebrated for reflecting Tasmania's natural beauty.
