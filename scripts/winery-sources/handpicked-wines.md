## Founding & History
Handpicked Wines was founded by William Dong, who initially began his involvement in the wine industry as a négociant. Dong later transitioned to vineyard ownership, acquiring the first Handpicked vineyard, Capella Vineyard on the Mornington Peninsula, in 2013. The company has since expanded its holdings to six vineyards across four Australian regions: Mornington Peninsula, Yarra Valley, Tasmania, and Barossa Valley. In addition to their owned vineyards, Handpicked Wines collaborates with growers in other Australian and international wine regions. A significant milestone occurred in October 2023 when Handpicked Wines finalized the acquisition of House of Arras from Accolade Wines. This acquisition included the House of Arras brand, 24 hectares of premium vineyards, inventory, and the Bay of Fires winery and cellar door located in Pipers River, Tasmania.

## Vineyard & Site
Handpicked Wines owns vineyards in Tasmania, specifically the Native Point Vineyard and Auburn Road Vineyard. The Pipers River region, where these vineyards are located, is characterized by a cool climate influenced by cool winds from the Bass Strait, which contributes to a long growing season. The soils in Pipers River are diverse, including rich volcanic soils, red ferrosol soils, and shallower, well-draining ironstone gravels. They are also described as deep, free-draining, friable soils over sandstones and siltstones, and alluvial deposits rich in minerals. These soils, particularly those high in iron and clay, offer good water retention. The region features rolling hills, with north-facing slopes and mid-to-higher altitudes being favorable for viticulture. The primary varietals planted by Handpicked Wines in Tasmania include Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and Riesling. The company employs environmentally sustainable principles and organic practices in its farming approach, such as mulching, the use of compost teas, and the reduction or elimination of chemicals and herbicides. Herbicides are no longer used in their vineyards.

## Cellar Door Experience
Handpicked Wines operates a cellar door in Pipers River, Tasmania, which is co-located with their "sister" brand, House of Arras. The cellar door is situated amidst lush woodlands along the banks of the Pipers River. Visitors are required to book tasting experiences in advance. Available experiences include the "Handpicked Masterclass - Vertical & Range Tasting," a 90-minute session conducted in the cellar door, vineyard, and winery, which delves into organic and sustainable winemaking practices, terroir, and regionality. Another option is the "Tasmanian Explorer Tasting," a 45-minute guided, seated experience featuring soil samples and interactive displays, highlighting Handpicked's Tasmanian vineyards and House of Arras wines. The cellar door is open from Thursday to Monday, with experiences available daily starting at 10 am. Guests can also enjoy walks along a boardwalk to observe native Tasmanian flora.

## Sustainability & Certifications
All six of Handpicked Wines' Australian vineyards, including Native Point Vineyard and Auburn Road Vineyard in Tasmania, are Certified Members of Sustainable Winegrowing Australia (SWA). The Native Point Vineyard and Auburn Road Vineyard in Tasmania also hold Organic Certification from the National Association for Sustainable Agriculture Australia (NASAA), making them the first Tasmanian vineyards to achieve both SWA and NASAA certifications. Sustainability practices implemented by Handpicked Wines include inter-row cropping for biodiversity, soil improvement, and weed control; the production and use of compost teas; mulching and composting to enhance soil health and conserve water; water harvesting and improved irrigation efficiency; integrated pest management; and mechanical weed control, with herbicides no longer in use in their vineyards. The company is also a certified member of the Australian Packaging Covenant Organisation (APCO) since 2021, achieving an 'Advanced' performance report in 2023 for its efforts in reducing packaging waste. They engage in recycling crushed glass bottles, reusing Prosecco bottles as water carafes, and utilizing recycled cardboard for packaging.

## Notable / Distinctive
Handpicked Wines is distinguished by its multi-regional operational model, which involves owning vineyards in key Australian regions like Mornington Peninsula, Yarra Valley, Tasmania, and Barossa Valley, alongside collaborations with growers in other areas. This approach aims to produce wines that are highly expressive of their specific regional and site characteristics. A particularly notable aspect is their acquisition of House of Arras, Australia's most awarded sparkling wine label, which significantly enhances their portfolio and reputation in the sparkling wine sector. The company's strong commitment to sustainability is also a key differentiator, evidenced by all six of their Australian vineyards being Sustainable Winegrowing Australia Certified, and their Tasmanian vineyards (Native Point and Auburn Road) additionally holding NASAA Organic Certification. The Pipers River cellar door offers a unique combined experience with House of Arras, allowing visitors to explore both still and sparkling wines in a single location. The cellar door itself is noted for its picturesque setting within lush woodlands along the Pipers River, providing opportunities for scenic walks.
