## Founding & History
Highclare Estate is situated on an elevated, north-facing hill in Tasmania's Tamar Valley. The site was an abandoned sheep farm, lying fallow for approximately 40 years until 2016. Sarah and Matt Hurst visited the property in 2016 and subsequently nurtured the land into a single-origin vineyard. Highclare Estate was established in 2018. It is a family-owned and run vineyard.

## People
Highclare Estate is owned and operated by Sarah and Matt Hurst. Matt Hurst, a Town Planner by profession, also serves as the viticulturalist. Sarah Hurst is an advertising executive with a Bachelor of Interior Architecture. The winemaking is handled by Fran Austin and Shane Holloway of Delamere Wines.

## Vineyard & Site
The vineyard is located at Hillwood in Tasmania's Tamar Valley, on an elevated, north-facing slope. The site is characterized by clean air and pure rain. Highclare Estate operates as a single-origin vineyard. The vineyard spans 8.5 hectares and contains 33,000 vines. The farming approach involves nurturing the land in harmony with the natural environment and wildlife. Varietals planted include Pinot Noir, Pinot Gris, and Chardonnay (for sparkling wine production).

## Cellar Door Experience
The Highclare Estate Wine Room, serving as the cellar door, is located at 68 Tamar Street, Launceston. This location is in the Launceston CBD, directly opposite the City Park gates. Visitors can drop in for guided tastings, enjoy a glass of wine, or purchase bottles to take home. The Wine Room is open every day from midday, and no bookings are required for tastings. The team aims to provide a relaxed and welcoming experience, sharing stories behind each vintage. The vineyard itself, located at 10 Johnstons Road, Hillwood, requires appointments for visits.

## Food & Hospitality
The Highclare Estate Wine Room offers DIY Charcuterie Platters, allowing guests to build their own or have staff select items. Additionally, Highclare Estate provides accommodation with the Highclare Townhouse, a two-bedroom apartment situated above the Wine Room in Launceston. This apartment can accommodate up to four guests.

## Awards & Recognition
Highclare Estate's first release of 2023 vintage wines received 93 Halliday points for both their Sparkling and Pinot Gris. The Halliday Wine Companion has awarded specific wines: the Highclare Estate The People's Share Pinot Noir 2024 received 93 silver points, while the Highclare Estate Sparkling Vintage Rosé 2024 and Highclare Estate The Folly Block Pinot Gris 2024 both received 92 silver points.
