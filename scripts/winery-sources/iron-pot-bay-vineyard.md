## Founding & History
Iron Pot Bay Vineyard was established in 1988 by Roderick Cuthbert and Kyra Hinman. Roderick Cuthbert, an innovator with an engineering background, won awards for his solutions to vineyard problems, including importing industrial fans to combat frost in the Rowella area. Kyra Hinman was responsible for vineyard operations and was instrumental in establishing the vineyard's early reputation, particularly as a pioneer of unwooded Chardonnay in Tasmania. Kyra Hinman passed away in 1995. The vineyard is now carried forward by Julieanne and Ashli Mani.

## People
The current owners of Iron Pot Bay Vineyard are Julieanne Mani, who serves as the vigneron and owner, and her daughter Ashli Mani, who is the winemaker and viticulturist. Ashli Mani studied Viticulture and Oenology in New Zealand. Both Julieanne and Ashli are often present at the vineyard, involved in various operations from vineyard work to cellar door tastings.

## Vineyard & Site
Iron Pot Bay Vineyard is located at Rowella, on the western side of the Tamar River. The vineyard was first planted in 1988. There is conflicting information regarding the vineyard size; some sources state it is 7.5 hectares, while others indicate 10 hectares of land are under vine. The grape varieties grown include Pinot Gris, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Semillon, Gewürztraminer, Riesling, Pinot Meunier, and Pinot Noir. The farming approach includes hand-picking and hand-pruning, reflecting a commitment to sustainability.

## Cellar Door Experience
The cellar door at Iron Pot Bay Vineyard is housed in a 1907 cottage overlooking the vines. Tastings are described as relaxed and personal, often hosted by Julieanne or Ashli Mani. Visitors can taste current Iron Pot Bay and Ashli Rose releases at an unhurried pace, with opportunities to hear stories about the wines, the vineyard, and the season. Guests are welcome to bring their own picnics and settle into the garden, and well-behaved dogs are also permitted. The cellar door is open Thursday to Saturday, from 11 am to 4 pm, and is closed on public holidays. Group tastings and tours can be arranged by enquiry. The cottage itself has Art Nouveau interior timber elements, fireplaces in every room, and a peaceful garden with hundred-year-old trees.
