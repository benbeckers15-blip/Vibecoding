## Founding & History
Jansz Tasmania is a luxury sparkling wine house that has been dedicated to sparkling winemaking since 1986. The original vineyard, established in 1975, was named Heemskerk, after Abel Tasman's ship. In 1985, Heemskerk partnered with the esteemed Champagne house Louis Roederer to produce Tasmania's first premium sparkling wine, named Jansz, honoring Abel Janszoon Tasman. Louis Roederer recognized the similarities between the Pipers River climate and the Champagne region.

The Hill-Smith family purchased Jansz in 1997 or 1998. Since then, the Hill-Smith family has invested in vineyards across Tasmania and grown Jansz Tasmania's reputation as a world-class Australian sparkling wine brand. The winery uses the traditional Méthode Champenoise for its vintage wines. Jansz Tasmania has coined the term "Méthode Tasmanoise®" for its approach to producing luxury sparkling wine, which emphasizes minimal intervention and respect for the Tasmanian environment. In 2012, the Hill-Smith family acquired the Parish vineyard in the Coal River Valley in southern Tasmania. In 2021, Jansz Tasmania opened a new winery at the family-owned Pontos Hills Vineyard estate in the Coal River Valley.

## People
Jansz Tasmania is owned by the Hill-Smith family. Robert Hill-Smith is the proprietor.

Teresa Heuzenroeder is the senior sparkling winemaker at Jansz Tasmania, appointed to the role in January 2025. She has over 30 years of experience in crafting award-winning cool-climate wines, specializing in sparkling and white winemaking across regions like Tasmania, Adelaide Hills, Eden Valley, and King Valley. Heuzenroeder has a scientific background and an eye for detail, and her career has included roles at Hill-Smith Family Estates, Petaluma, and Croser.

## Vineyard & Site
Jansz Tasmania's primary vineyard is located in the Pipers River region, within the Tamar Valley in northeast Tasmania. This area is sometimes referred to as 'Sparkling Tasmania' due to its cool climate. The climate in Pipers River is moderated by its close proximity to the Bass Strait, with ocean breezes helping to minimize frost risk in winter and keep temperatures down in summer, allowing grapes to ripen slowly and develop intense flavors. The vineyard rests on red, free-draining basalt soils.

The Jansz Estate Vineyard consists of 15 hectares of Chardonnay, 12 hectares of Pinot Noir, and 3 hectares of Pinot Meunier. These varietals and their ratios correspond to those typically used in Champagne production. Jansz also sources fruit from vineyards in southern Tasmania, specifically the Coal River Valley, which the Hill-Smith family purchased in 2012. The winery also has vineyards in White Hills. The company produces wine in the ultra-cool Pipers River region, where the average maximum temperature rarely exceeds 20°C (68°F).

## Cellar Door Experience
The Jansz Tasmania Wine Room is located adjacent to the vineyard, overlooking a picturesque lake. It offers guests the opportunity to taste the Jansz Tasmania collection. Visitors can be guided through a curated tasting. Bookings are strongly encouraged, especially for intimate and frequently visited experiences. Group tastings for 8 or more people are hosted by a wine ambassador and include a tasting of four wines from the collection, lasting approximately one hour. The Wine Room also functions as an interpretive center where visitors can learn about winemaking in the region and Tasmanian history.

## Awards & Recognition
The Jansz Tasmania Premium Sparkling Cuvée NV has received multiple accolades, including 94 points from Decanter and Ned Goodwin, and 93 points from Wine Enthusiast. Ned Goodwin describes it as a quality domestic sparkling with excellent integration, creamy yet fresh, and rich yet ethereal. Wine Enthusiast noted it as a blend of Champagne's three classic varieties, with fruit from both north and south of the island, and 18 months on lees resulting in a fruit-driven bubbly with brioche notes. Tyson Stelzer, a Champagne expert, once referred to the Premium Vintage Rosé 2007 as a 'poor man's Krug rosé'.

## Sustainability & Certifications
Jansz Tasmania is committed to sustainable practices. Their "Méthode Tasmanoise" philosophy embraces respect for the environment, natural wilderness, clean air, fertile soils, and pristine waters, and emphasizes minimal intervention.
