## Founding & History
Leven Valley Vineyard's Chardonnay and Pinot Noir vines were initially planted in 1997 by Steve and Diana Usher. The first commercial vintage was released in 2001 under the "Moonrakers" label. In 2003, the label was changed to Leven Valley Vineyard, a name chosen to reflect the region. The vineyard's vintages have received multiple awards at the Hobart Wine Show. In 2012, Wendy and Mike Dontschuk acquired the vineyard, focusing on producing high-quality wines. Terri and Trenton Seager purchased Leven Valley Vineyard in early 2022, and they are dedicated to nurturing the vines.

## People
The current owners of Leven Valley Vineyard are Terri and Trenton Seager, who acquired the property in early 2022. They are actively involved in tending the vines to ensure optimal grape quality. Previously, Wendy and Mike Dontschuk owned the vineyard from 2012, with their wines being handmade locally by Neil Colbeck with their assistance. Terri Seager also presents wine and chocolate tasting experiences at the cellar door.

## Vineyard & Site
Leven Valley Vineyard consists of 1.5 hectares of vines. The vineyard is situated on a warm, north-facing slope in Gunns Plains, North West Tasmania, overlooking the Leven River. This location is approximately 20 minutes south of Ulverstone. The terroir is characterized by deep red and black clay loams over limestone. This specific site contributes to Leven Valley Vineyard being one of Australia's latest ripening vineyards, producing cool-climate characteristics in its wines. The primary varietals planted are Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, first established in 1997. The vintages are hand-picked by friends, local residents, and volunteers.

## Cellar Door Experience
The Leven Valley Vineyard Cellar Door is located on a north-facing hill in Gunns Plains, offering panoramic views of the Leven River. It is open from September to April, Wednesday to Saturday, 10 am to 5 pm. From May to June, it is open Friday and Saturday, 10 am to 5 pm. Visits outside these hours can be arranged by appointment. The cellar door is designed to showcase their estate-grown, handcrafted cool-climate wines. The tasting fee is $10 per person, though one source indicates $15 per person. Visitors can enjoy a glass of wine and are welcome to bring their own nibbles or picnic to enjoy in the garden. The cellar door may restrict the number of visitors at any given time for safety.

## Food & Hospitality
Leven Valley Vineyard does not have an on-site restaurant or kitchen. However, the cellar door provides facilities for visitors to bring their own nibbles or picnic to enjoy in the garden. BBQ facilities are available on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to availability, allowing guests to bring their own winter barbecue ingredients. They host special "Off Season Experiences" which can include platters of Tasmanian produce or wine and chocolate tastings. The wine and chocolate tasting is presented by owner Terri Seager and Jessica Tivendale of Chokolate Journeys, pairing Leven Valley Vineyard's cold climate wines with bean-to-bar chocolate.
