## Founding & History
Marion's Vineyard was established in 1979 by Marion and Mark Semmens, who were pioneers of the Tamar River valley wine region. They purchased a 14-hectare rundown apple orchard on the Tamar River in 1980. The couple, an immigrant from Cyprus and a Californian, had no prior background in winemaking but were impressed by Tasmanian wines during a holiday. They initially planted 4 hectares of vines. Marion's Vineyard is noted for opening the first cellar door in the north of Tasmania. The family still owns the vineyard, with the second and third generations involved.

## People
The current owners include Marion Semmens, her daughter Cynthea Semmens, Cynthea's husband David Feldheim, and Cynthea's brother Nick. Cynthea Semmens is the principal winemaker. She grew up on the vineyard, helping to plant early vines, and later earned a wine marketing degree from Roseworthy and an oenology degree from Charles Sturt University. After gaining international winemaking experience, she returned to Tasmania in 2010 with her winemaker husband, David Feldheim. Nick Semmens works in the vines. Marion Semmens is often present at the cellar door.

## Vineyard & Site
Marion's Vineyard is situated on the west bank of the Tamar River, approximately 35 kilometers north of Launceston, in the village of Deviot. The site was originally a 14-hectare apple orchard. Currently, there are 7.8 hectares under vine. The vineyard features an amphitheatre-shaped property with vines arrayed on a slope leading to the river. The Tamar River (Kanamaluka) at the base of the property provides refractive light, aiding photosynthesis and allowing for the ripening of red varietals beyond Pinot Noir.

Initial plantings in 1980 included Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Cabernet Sauvignon, and Müller-Thurgau. The vineyard has since expanded its varietals to include Cabernet Franc, Syrah, Pinot Gris, Muscat, Mavrodaphne, Zinfandel, Gewürztraminer, Tempranillo, and Cascade, with the last vines planted in 2003. The vineyard has been in organic and biodynamic transition since 2018, achieving Organic Certification in 2021 and biodynamic certification in 2022.

## Cellar Door Experience

Visitors can customize their own platters and enjoy wine with views of the Tamar Valley. Reservations are essential for some experiences, but visitors can also drop by. The cellar door has a cozy fire in winter. Well-socialized pets are welcome.

## Awards & Recognition
Marion's Vineyard received biodynamic certification in 2022, making it only the second vineyard on the "Apple Isle" (Tasmania) to achieve this. Organic Certification was awarded in 2021. James Halliday's Australian Wine Companion lists Marion's Vineyard with a rating and notes specific wines like the Marion's Vineyard Blanc de Blancs Traditional Method 2019 (91 silver) and Marion's Vineyard Estate Chardonnay 2022 (93 silver).

## Sustainability & Certifications
Marion's Vineyard has been in organic and biodynamic transition since 2018. It achieved Organic Certification in 2021 and biodynamic certification in 2022. The vineyard is fully certified organic and biodynamic, aiming to produce wines that reflect the land and respect it. The farming approach involves maximum input to the soil and minimal intervention in the winery.
