## Founding & History
Moores Hill Estate is a family-operated wine producer located in the Tamar Valley, Tasmania. The vineyard was established in 1997, with the first vines planted that year. Wines have been produced from the estate since 2003. Fiona Weller and Julian Allport purchased Moores Hill in 2008, transitioning it from a basic farmgate operation to a premium wine business. A significant milestone was the construction of their on-site, 100% solar-powered winery in 2017.

## People
Moores Hill Estate is a family-owned vineyard. Fiona Weller is the Co-Owner and Manager, while her husband, Julian Allport, is the winemaker. Julian Allport emphasizes small-batch winemaking to maximize quality and complexity.

## Vineyard & Site
The vineyard is situated on a northeast-facing hillside near Exeter, at an elevation of 120 meters above sea level. This site is part of a ridge system that provides good aspect, elevation, and drainage. The vineyard benefits from a microclimate that allows fruit to ripen slowly and evenly. The site is approximately 5 km from the Tamar River and 30 km from Bass Strait.

Currently, Moores Hill Estate has 5.6 hectares under vine. The original vineyard plantings in 1997 included Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Riesling, and small amounts of Cabernet and Merlot. In 2015, an additional 2 hectares of Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and Pinot Gris were planted. The current plantings include 1.0ha of Pinot Noir, 1.0ha of Chardonnay, 0.5ha of Cabernet Sauvignon, 0.5ha of Merlot, and 1.5ha of Riesling. The vineyard is fully irrigated by a dam and a bore, though irrigation is typically only needed during exceptionally dry summers. Viticultural practices involve preventative management of diseases and pests. The vineyard is covered entirely with nets in February to protect fruit from birds. Grapes are picked entirely by hand, with harvest typically commencing in March for sparkling Chardonnay, and finishing in early May with later-ripening varieties like Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot. The winery has plans to convert to organic farming.

## Cellar Door Experience
The cellar door and tasting room overlook the sloping vineyard. It is open six days a week, from 11 am to 5 pm, and is closed on Christmas Day, New Year's Day, and Good Friday. Tastings are offered, along with wine by the glass or bottle. A "Whole Range" tasting of five wines costs $15 per person, which is refundable. Bookings are not essential for general visits, but they are appreciated for groups of six or more, with a maximum group size of 12 during cooler months. The cellar door is accessible for those in wheelchairs or who are physically impaired. Visitors can enjoy vineyard views from the verandah.

## Food & Hospitality
Moores Hill Estate is not a full restaurant but offers delicious tasting plates featuring Tasmanian produce to share with wine. Options include a seasonal Grazing Board, a selection of cheeses, or a Seafood Plate. Fresh oysters are also available. Coffee, beer, and soft drinks are served. Food is served until 4 pm.

## Sustainability & Certifications
Moores Hill Estate operates Tasmania's first 100% solar-powered winery, built in 2017. The winery uses 108 solar panels and 80kw of battery storage, making it entirely off-grid for power. All water for the winery is collected from the roof, and all winery waste is treated on-site through a closed-loop waste management system. The estate has Sustainable Tourism Accreditation by ATIC. They have plans to convert to organic farming.
