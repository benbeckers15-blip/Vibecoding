## Founding & History
Riversdale Estate was first settled in 1824 by James Belbin Sr., a "convict settler" who received land grants between Hobart and Pittwater. Belbin built the original Riversdale homestead, the foundations and flooring of which remain today. The farming lands were reawakened in 1919 when Private Douglas Denholm, an ANZAC 'Soldier Settler,' was granted the land, establishing a farm known for prize-winning sheep. After Denholm's death in 1965, the land remained untouched until 1980 when it was purchased by 26-year-old Ian Roberts. Roberts initially ran the property as an Angora goat farm, achieving international acclaim for his Angora stud programs. In 1991, Ian and his wife Wendy planted the first vineyards and a large olive grove, making them among the first in the Coal River Valley. The Riversdale Estate wine label was launched in 2008, initially exclusively through Cellarmasters, after their grapes, grown for other brands, began winning medals. In 2019, the winery completed a $7 million sustainable, state-of-the-art facility named 'Celestia,' featuring cutting-edge French technology.

## People
The current owners of Riversdale Estate are Ian and Wendy Roberts. Their three sons, Christiaan, Anton, and Rainier, are involved in the family enterprise. Rainier Roberts serves as the vigneron. Marais, originally from South Africa, resides in the historic Old Homestead on the property with his family. Christiaan and Anton Roberts continue a family law practice in Sydney, while Rainier is pursuing a double degree in law and viticulture.

## Vineyard & Site
Riversdale Estate is situated in the basin of the Coal River Valley in Tasmania. It is the only vineyard in the region to front Pittwater, which is a significant component of its unique micro-climate. The property's proximity to Pittwater suggests it was once underwater, resulting in diverse soil profiles, nutrient deposits, and mineral trace elements, including seabed rocks and stone found up to 1km from the water at depths of 1-2 meters. The vineyard is north-facing and uninterrupted, ensuring consistent all-day sun during the growing seasons, which is integral for high-quality fruit production. The site features unique dolerite soils. Since 1991, 40 hectares have been selectively planted across various areas of the property. Afternoon sea breezes from Frederick Henry Bay provide optimal airflow and a natural buffer against frost, contributing to consistent yields.
