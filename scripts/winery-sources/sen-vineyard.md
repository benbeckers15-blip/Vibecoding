## Founding & History
Sen Vineyard was established in 2018 in the White Hills District, located within Tasmania's Tamar Valley. The founder, Balram 'Samir' Sen, first envisioned owning acreage in this area after flying over the property in 1995. The opportunity to plant vines arose in 2018 with the introduction of an irrigation scheme, and a friend suggested combining architecture with agriculture on the site. The vineyard is situated on Kanhaika Farm, a 50-acre property.

## People
The current owner and founder of Sen Vineyard is Balram 'Samir' Sen. Samir's background includes a career as a marine engineer, where he became a leader in his field and served as Chief Engineer at Woodside Energy, having moved to Tasmania from Fiji in 1995. The wines are produced at Frogmore Creek Winery in Cambridge, Tasmania, where their winemaker is responsible for crafting the style, flavour, and personality of the finished wines. Samir works with a team, and individuals named Sammi, Josie, and Romi have been mentioned in connection with hospitality and events.

## Vineyard & Site
Sen Vineyard has 2.5 hectares planted with grapes. The varietals cultivated include 1 hectare of Pinot Noir, 1 hectare of Chardonnay, and 0.5 hectare of Pinot Gris. These vines were planted during the 2018-2019 vintage and represent quintessential Tasmanian cool climate varietals. The vineyard features different clones of Pinot Noir, Chardonnay clones 95 and 76, and three distinct clones of Pinot Gris. The site is characterized by rich, deep black loamy soils and a microclimate that contributes to an extended growing season and hang time. Sen Vineyard is perched on top of the valley in the White Hills District, described as an "eagle's nest" on Kanhaika Farm, flanked by two mountain ranges with Ben Lomond in the background. It offers views overlooking the city of Launceston and its surroundings. The vineyard employs regenerative farming practices.
