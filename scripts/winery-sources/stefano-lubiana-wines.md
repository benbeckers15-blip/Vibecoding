## Founding & History
Stefano Lubiana Wines was established in 1990 by fifth-generation winemaker Steve Lubiana and his wife, Monique. The couple moved to Tasmania in 1990 after Steve, who grew up in South Australia's Riverland region, sought a location to produce premium cool-climate wines, particularly sparkling wine. They purchased a 252-hectare parcel of land in Granton, Derwent Valley, 20 kilometers north of Hobart, and planted the first vines, primarily Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, in the spring of 1991. Initially, they sold most of their fruit to other wineries to support themselves. The winery began its conversion to biodynamic practices in 2010 and achieved full certification in 2013, becoming Tasmania's first certified biodynamic vineyard. In 2015, the Lubianas expanded by purchasing the Panorama vineyard in the Huon Valley, which was in conversion to biodynamic certification as of 2021.

## People
The winery is owned and operated by Steve and Monique Lubiana. Steve Lubiana is a fifth-generation winemaker, with his family's winemaking roots tracing back to Trieste in Northern Italy. Their son, Marco Lubiana, has followed in the family tradition, becoming a sixth-generation winemaker and serving as the vineyard manager.

## Vineyard & Site
The Stefano Lubiana vineyard in Granton, Derwent Valley, occupies just over 28 hectares. Some sources state the vineyard size as 25 hectares. The property is north-facing and overlooks the Derwent River estuary. Steve Lubiana selected the site for its soil, frost-free location, dry autumn conditions, and disease-free environment, noting his preference for poor soils to control vine vigor in a cool climate. The site contains various soil types, including terra rossa, gravels, and loams. In some sections, the soils are becoming darker due to increased organic matter.

The vineyard is predominantly planted with Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Other varietals planted include Pinot Gris, Riesling, Sauvignon Blanc, Syrah (Shiraz), Merlot, Malbec, Malvasia, and Blaufränkisch. Most of the vineyard is planted at a density of 4,500 vines per hectare, with a close-planted 1.3-hectare block established in 2019 at 11,000 vines per hectare.

The farming approach is certified biodynamic, a practice the winery has cultivated since 2009, with full certification achieved in 2013. This involves excluding synthetic chemicals, using composts, and conducting vineyard activities in tune with the lunar calendar. Mid-row cover crops include beans and legumes, and treated pine posts have been replaced with steel since 2010.

## Cellar Door Experience
The cellar door is located in a Tuscan-inspired building set amongst the vines, offering views down to the Derwent River. Wine tastings are by appointment only and are priced at $25 per person for a 40-minute session from a daily selection. Bookings are essential and can be made via their website. The cellar door is closed on public holidays. Visitors can enjoy a glass of wine in front of an open fire or play bocce. The wines offered are biodynamic, organic, and vegan.

## Food & Hospitality
Stefano Lubiana Wines features an Italian restaurant, Osteria, which offers a unique dining experience with a focus on modern Australian cuisine. The Osteria's menu is fresh, seasonal, and inspired by regional Italian dishes, primarily based on produce from their biodynamically run vegetable garden. They also harvest honey from their bees, pickle and preserve produce, and make their own bread from organic flours in a wood-fired oven. Ethically raised free-range meats from local farmers and wild-caught seafood from Tasmania's waters are used. Even the coffee beans and milk are certified organic and biodynamic. The menu changes weekly, and bookings are advised. The Osteria is open Wednesday to Sunday from 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Chef Brendon Walter previously cooked for Frogmore Creek Wines and leads the kitchen. The restaurant offers a set lunch menu, with a chef's menu priced at $65. The winery does not currently offer event or wedding packages.

## Awards & Recognition
Stefano Lubiana Wines has received significant recognition. The winery is rated 5 red stars in James Halliday's Australian Wine Companion. Their Pinot Noirs have won the International Wine Challenge Biodynamic Trophy on three separate occasions, recognized as the "Best Biodynamic Wine in the World". James Suckling awarded the La Roccia 2022 Single Block Pinot Noir 99 points. Decanter's Andrew Jefford named Steve Lubiana as one of Australia's ten bravest winemakers in May 2010. JancisRobinson.com has given scores such as 18/20 for a Pinot Noir (vintage not specified) and 16.5+/20 for the 2016 vintage.

## Sustainability & Certifications
Stefano Lubiana Wines is Tasmania's first certified biodynamic vineyard and winery. They began converting the vineyard in late 2009 and achieved full certification through Australia Certified Organic (ACO) in 2013. The biodynamic approach, inspired by Rudolph Steiner's philosophy, involves excluding synthetic chemicals and pesticides, using composts, and aligning vineyard activities with the lunar calendar. The winery stopped using chemical fertilizers in 2001 and synthetic herbicides and fungicides on a third of their vineyard from winter 2008. They started applying biodynamic soil spray Preparation 500 in 2008. The benefits of their biodynamic practices include deeper, more fertile soils with increased biodiversity, improved water holding capacity, and tougher, more resilient vines that resist negative climatic conditions. Winery wastewater is treated and reused, and all grape waste and marc are composted. Treated pine posts have not been used since 2010, with new plantings and replacements made of steel. Their wines are also vegan.

Stefano Lubiana Wines is distinctive as Tasmania's first certified biodynamic vineyard and winery, achieving this accreditation in 2013. This commitment to biodynamics, including the use of cosmic rhythms for vine care and soil regeneration, sets them apart. The winery is recognized for its consistent production of "precise" and "characterful" wines. Their Pinot Noirs have won the International Wine Challenge Biodynamic Trophy three times.
