## Founding & History
Viridian Wines is a small, family-owned label established by Michael and Lyn Rochford. The Rochford family did not have a prior background or family history in winegrowing. The first grapes at their Broadview vineyard were planted in 1996, and plantings were gradually expanded to reach two hectares. The name "Viridian" is derived from the color green, associated with the Environmentally Sustainable Design movement, reflecting the winery's aim to produce premium wines using environmentally sustainable techniques.

## People
Viridian Wines is owned by Lyn and Michael Rochford. Lyn Rochford holds a post-graduate qualification in Viticulture and Wine Technology. The wines are made by Alain Rousseau at Frogmore Creek Wines.

## Vineyard & Site
The vineyard, named Broadview, is a tiny two-hectare site. It is situated near the larger vineyards of Stefano Lubiana and Derwent Estate, overlooking the Derwent River, approximately 20km north of Hobart. The site is characterized by sloping tertiary limestone rubble that descends towards wetlands on the edge of the Derwent River. The grapes benefit from the interplay of sunshine, rain, and regular fogs in the valley. Viridian Wines grows Riesling, Pinot Noir, and Chardonnay grapes. The vineyard employs environmentally sustainable practices, including hand pruning and hand harvesting of grapes.
